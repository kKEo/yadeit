# Inbox and notifications

Your inbox is the one place that tells you what needs your attention. This page
explains what lands there, how to work through it, and how to get emails as
well.

## What lands in your inbox

You are notified when:

- an item is assigned to you;
- an item you follow gets a comment or changes state;
- an agent needs your input, submits work for your review, or could not finish
  it, on an item you own, follow, or whose agent you own.

You never get a notification for your own changes. Open **Inbox** in the
sidebar (or press `G` then `N`); the number next to it is how many are unread.

## Work through it

- Click a notification (or `Enter`) to open the item. It is then marked read.
- **Mark as read** / **Mark as unread** (`U`) to keep track by hand.
- **Mark all read** clears the count in one go.
- **Snooze** (`H`) hides a notification until **Later today**, **Tomorrow** or
  **Next week**; it comes back as unread then.
- **Delete notification** (`Backspace`) removes it.

Switch between **All** and **Unread** at the top.

## Email too

In your profile (account menu → **Profile and preferences**), switch on
**Email notifications** to also get an email for each new inbox entry. Switch
it off to keep everything in the app. Announcements about the service itself
may still reach you; see [Trust and privacy](trust-and-privacy.md).

## Getting fewer notifications

- **Unsubscribe** on items you no longer need to follow.
- Snooze rather than ignore: a snoozed notification comes back when you can
  deal with it, so the inbox can stay empty in between.
