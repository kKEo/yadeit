# Quick start

This page takes you from no account to a small project with your first items
on a board. It takes about ten minutes. You need an email address you can open
right now.

## 1. Create your account (2 minutes)

1. Open the Yadeit address you were given and choose **Create a free account**
   (or **Create an account** under the sign-in form).
2. Enter your name, your email and a password of at least 8 characters, then
   choose **Create account**.
3. Open the email from Yadeit and follow the link in it. The page says **Your
   email address is confirmed.** Choose **Continue to Yadeit**.

> **Note.** You can look around before you confirm your email, but you cannot
> invite anyone until you do. A yellow bar at the top reminds you, and has a
> **Resend confirmation email** button if the email did not arrive.

> **Tip.** Were you invited by someone? Open the link in the invitation email
> instead. It takes you to their workspace, and the account you create there
> joins it straight away.

## 2. Create your workspace (1 minute)

A workspace is your team's own space: its projects, its people, its settings.

1. On the **Welcome** screen, type a **Workspace name**, for example the name
   of your team or company.
2. Yadeit suggests a **Web address** from the name. Keep it or change it; it
   becomes part of every link, like `/w/acme`.
3. Choose **Create workspace**.

Yadeit puts a sample project called **Getting started** (key `DEMO`) into the
new workspace, with three items that explain themselves. Open
`DEMO-1` to see what an item looks like. You can leave the sample project
alone once you have your own; nothing depends on it.

## 3. Create a project (1 minute)

1. In the left sidebar, choose **Projects**, then **New project**.
2. Give it a **Name**, such as *Website*, and a short **Key**, such as `WEB`.
   Every item in the project gets a number under that key: `WEB-1`, `WEB-2`,
   and so on.
3. Choose **Create project**.

> **Warning.** The key cannot be changed later, so pick something short you
> are happy to see on every item.

## 4. Add a few items (3 minutes)

1. Press `C` anywhere in the workspace, or choose **New item** on the
   **Items** page.
2. Pick your project, type a title such as *Write the opening text*, and press
   `Enter`. The title is the only thing you must fill in.
3. Tick **Create more** to keep the form open, and add two or three more items.

Everything else (who does it, how urgent it is, a description) can be added
later.

## 5. Move work across the board (2 minutes)

1. On the **Items** page, switch the **Layout** to **Board**. Items are shown
   in columns by state: **Backlog**, **To do**, **In progress**, **Done**.
2. Drag one item from **To do** to **In progress**. That is how you say "I am
   working on this".
3. Click any item to open it in a side panel. Change its **Priority**, set the
   **Assignee** to yourself, and write a short description. Changes are saved
   as you make them.
4. Press `Escape` to close the panel.

## 6. Invite a teammate (1 minute)

1. In the sidebar, choose **Members**.
2. Under **Invite people**, type their email, choose a role (**Member** is
   right for most people) and **Send invitation**.

They get an email with a link that is valid for a few days.
