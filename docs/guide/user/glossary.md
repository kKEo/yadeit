# Glossary

The words this guide uses, in alphabetical order, each with what it means in
Yadeit. Where a page explains more, the entry links to it.

**Acceptance criteria.** The conditions that make an item done, usually kept
in its checklist. Agents tick them as they finish them.

**Activity.** An item's history: every change, by whom and when.

**Admin.** A workspace role that manages people and settings. See
[roles](workspaces-and-people.md#roles).

**Agent.** An AI assistant, script or automation that works in a workspace
like a member, owned by a person. See [Working with AI agents](agents.md).

**Agent run.** One stretch of work by an agent on an item, with its timeline
of thoughts, actions, questions and results.

**AI help.** The built-in AI features: drafting, splitting, summarising and
triage suggestions. See [Built-in AI help](ai-help.md).

**Assignee.** The one person responsible for an item.

**Audit log.** The record of who changed access, sharing and settings, and who
deleted what.

**Backlog.** Items worth doing some day but not planned yet.

**Blocked.** An item waiting for another item that is not finished.

**Board.** A layout that shows items as cards in columns.

**Claim.** What an agent does to take an item, so nobody else takes it too.

**Command palette.** The search-and-command box opened with `Ctrl+K`.

**Cycle.** A fixed stretch of time a project plans in. See
[cycles](projects-and-planning.md#cycles).

**Delegate.** To hand an item to an agent with the **Agent** field.

**Demand.** How many pieces of feedback ask for an item.

**Doc.** A written page in Markdown, for a project or the whole workspace.

**Draft.** A doc only the team can see, not yet published.

**Feedback.** What people tell you they need, collected for triage.

**Guest.** A workspace role that can read and comment but not change work.

**Inbox.** Your personal list of notifications.

**Item.** One piece of work, called by its key, such as `WEB-14`.

**Key.** The short code of a project (`WEB`), and with a number the name of
an item (`WEB-14`).

**Kind.** What sort of work an item is: task, bug, feature, design or
research.

**Label.** A free tag on an item.

**Markdown.** A simple way to format text with symbols: `**bold**`, `- list`,
`[link](https://…)`.

**MCP (Model Context Protocol).** The standard way AI assistants connect to
tools. Agents use it to work in Yadeit.

**Member.** Anyone in a workspace; also the everyday role between admin and
guest.

**Needs input.** An item where an agent asked a question and waits for a
person's answer.

**Operator.** The person or company that runs a Yadeit installation.

**Owner.** The highest workspace role; can delete the workspace and change
its plan.

**Plan.** What a workspace is allowed: how many people, projects and AI
tokens.

**Priority.** How urgent an item is, from no priority to urgent.

**Project.** A group of related items under one key.

**Public form.** A project's feedback form that works without an account.

**Published.** A doc everyone in the workspace can see.

**Review.** The step where a person checks finished work before it is done.

**Saved view.** A named set of filters, layout, grouping and sorting.

**State.** Where an item is in its life, such as Todo or In progress. Every
state belongs to one of six fixed groups.

**Sub-item.** An item that is part of a bigger item, its parent.

**Token (access token).** A secret key that lets an agent act in the
workspace, with limits you choose.

**Token (AI).** The unit AI use is counted in; roughly a short word.

**Triage.** Deciding what to do with new input: accept, add to an item,
decline, mark as duplicate or snooze.

**Webhook.** A message Yadeit sends to another service when items change.

**Workspace.** A team's own space with its projects, people and settings.
