# Your account

This page covers what belongs to you rather than to a workspace: your profile,
language, email notifications, password, your data and deleting your account.
Open the account menu (top right of the app) and choose **Profile and
preferences**.

## Name and language

Change your name, pick **English** or **Polski** under **Language**, and
choose **Save**. The app switches language and remembers your choice on every
device you sign in from.

## Email notifications

Switch **Email notifications** on to get an email for each new inbox entry, or
off to see them only in the app. See [Inbox and notifications](inbox.md).

## Confirm your email address

Until you confirm your address, a bar at the top of the app reminds you, and
you cannot invite people. **Resend confirmation email** sends a new link; the
link in an older email may have expired.

## Forgotten password

On the sign-in page, choose **Forgot your password?**, enter your email and
**Send reset link**. Open the link in the email, choose a new password of at
least 8 characters and **Change password**. Then sign in as usual.

For your safety, the page says a link is on its way whether or not the address
has an account.

## Download your data

Under **Your data**, choose **Download my data**. You get a file with your
profile and everything you wrote, in every workspace you belong to.

## Delete your account

Under **Your data**, choose **Delete my account…**, enter **Your password** and
confirm. This is final:

- Your account is deleted and you are signed out.
- What you wrote in shared workspaces stays there, without your name.
- A workspace that only you are in is deleted too.
- Agents you own are removed.

Yadeit refuses when deleting would leave others stranded: if you are the only
owner of a workspace other people use, make someone else an owner first.

## Signing out

Account menu → **Sign out**. If you think someone else has your password,
change it with the reset link above.
